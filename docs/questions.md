# FasalBima Sathi — 10 Evaluation Questions (Day 5)

Questions Q1–Q6 come directly from the official project card (`SJ-07`), and Q7–Q10 cover additional real farmer scenarios from the PMFBY Operational Guidelines. **Q6 must always get an honest `"I don't know"` response.**

1. **Q1 (Project Card):** What premium do I pay for soybean crop insurance in kharif?
2. **Q2 (Project Card):** Is hailstorm damage covered for my individual farm?
3. **Q3 (Project Card):** How many days after harvest are cut-and-spread crops covered for unseasonal rain?
4. **Q4 (Project Card):** Hailstorm hit my 1.2 ha soybean at 9 pm yesterday. By when must I inform, through which channels, and what premium did I pay if the scale of finance is Rs 49,000 per ha?
5. **Q5 (Project Card):** How did the claim intimation time change between the 2016 guidelines and the revamped guidelines?
6. **Q6 (Project Card — Unanswerable):** Has my PMFBY claim for kharif 2025 been approved? *(Expected: honest `"I don't know"`)*
7. **Q7:** Is PMFBY crop insurance compulsory for farmers who have a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) loan?
8. **Q8:** What happens if deficit rainfall prevents farmers in my village from sowing at the start of the season?
9. **Q9:** Are losses due to war, nuclear risks, or malicious damage covered under PMFBY?
10. **Q10:** If my bank or insurer does not resolve my crop insurance dispute, where can I escalate a grievance?

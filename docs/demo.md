# Farmer walkthrough

[Watch the narrated demo on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SqBRUVHGCP8iX5eNJeH-B5WiEgFAyB5a/view)
· [Repository video](demo.mp4) · [Narration transcript](demo-narration.txt)
· [Live app](https://fasalbima-sathi.onrender.com/)

This 225-second edited walkthrough combines six actual screenshots captured
from the local Day 13 application on 21 November 2026 with ten explanatory
chapters. An expressive synthetic Indian English voice provides the narration.
It is not a human recording or a continuous live screen recording. Chapter
durations follow the explanation and do not represent application latency.

The narration covers:

1. The farmer's problem and three main actions.
2. Asking practical questions in English, Marathi or Hindi.
3. Official citations and boundaries on personal claim-status queries.
4. Premium inputs, area units and bank-provided scale of finance.
5. Reviewing and confirming before a calculation.
6. The soybean example: ₹58,800 cover and ₹1,176 maximum estimated premium.
7. Reporting deadlines and the live countdown for applicable cases.
8. The checklist and downloadable reporting note.
9. Language selection, feedback and starting a new conversation.
10. Independent guidance, privacy and contacting the insurer.

Some features, including source expansion, the active countdown, downloads and
feedback, are explained over related screens rather than demonstrated through
recorded clicks. The screenshots are actual app views; chapter headings and side
panels are editorial annotations, not additional application controls.

No real farmer information or credentials are shown. Live-provider performance
and safety checks are evidenced separately in the production benchmark and tests.
